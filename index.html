FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to know anything about tech?

No. I set everything up and show you how your dashboard works in a short call. If you can use email, you can use it.

What do I need to give you to start?

Your business name, logo (or I can design one), the services you offer, your hours and contact details, and any photos you have. I'll send a short checklist.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and the site, content and designs are yours.

What happens if I already have a website?

That's a redesign. I keep what works, move your content over, and make sure your Google listing points to the new site.

Are there monthly costs?

Only what the providers charge you directly: your domain and your email account. The Care Plan is optional.

How do appointments work?

Clients pick a time on your website. You get the request in your dashboard and by email, then approve or decline it with one tap.

Can you work with businesses outside Toronto?

Yes. Everything is done online, by video call and email.
